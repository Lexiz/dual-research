Deliverable 02 · Component library · Material mode

Named, slotted, copy-pasteable. Every template builds from these.

A card is not a rectangle the generator draws — it is a slotted component the generator fills. Reserved positions for the icon, the status dot, the footer line. Material mode keeps the same library as Pixel (7 cards, 4 chips, 6 nodes, stages, lanes, groups, callouts) so a downstream generator switching modes only swaps token values, not component names.

How to read these specs. Each card shows a true-pixel rendering on the left and the slot anatomy on the right. The dimensions, paddings, and slot positions are locked — fill the slots, don't move them. Optional slots stay empty if unused; the card geometry doesn't reflow.

02.0Theme behavior across components

Every primitive on this page exists in both light and dark theme. Geometry, slots, paddings, slot positions are identical — only token values swap. The categorical surfaces (--ds-surface-sql, --ds-surface-primary, etc.) keep their id across themes; the gradient stops inside change. The two filter ids (cardShadow, cardShadowDark) stay the same; their bodies rewrite per theme — see foundations §01.5.

Primitive groupTheme behavior
Cards · all 7 variantsIdentical layout. Surface tokens swap per foundations §01.2. Light cards (card.secondary, card.reference, card.compact) lose their drop shadow in dark — they get a 1px rgba(255,255,255,0.10) stroke instead. Dark categorical cards keep their drop shadow (deeper flood, #000000 at 0.55).
Chips · 4 typesIdentical. M3 chip default is pill (--md-shape-full). chip.status dot colors are V2 palette tokens — theme-portable. chip.count and chip.label use the light/dark card tokens depending on the card they sit on.
Shapes · 6 nodesIdentical geometry. node.note has a theme-specific fill: light = warm sable-tinted #f4ecd9; dark = warm near-black #2a2620. The amber stroke (--p-warn #d4a056) stays in both — it's what makes the note read as a note.
Stages · numbered tokensIdentical. Number circle uses the theme-portable accent (#6b9cf0). Stage label ink swaps from --ink-4 to --ink-4-dark.

02.1Cards · anatomy

Every card has three locked slots: icon (top-left, 36 × 36 with 20/20 inset), status (top-right, 12 × 12), footer (bottom-left, italic ink-3). Slot positions don't move between variants. If a slot is unused, it stays empty — the geometry doesn't reflow.

Two-step icon pattern. Always draw the 36 × 36 slot rect FIRST at (card.x + 20, card.y + 20), then wrap the icon ART in <g transform="translate(card.x+38, card.y+38)"> (the slot center) with all shape coordinates relative to (0, 0). The slot rect is the load-bearing geometry — without it, hand-computed per-icon offsets compound into baseline drift across same-rank cards. See troubleshooting entry "Icons drift / sit at different baselines".

Color & sizing rules

  • Categorical-filled shapes (circle, hexagon) use one of the 7 --ds-surface-* gradients — never an improvised fill.
  • Outlined shapes (pill, diamond) use --surface-light + --rule-strong border in light theme; --surface-light-dark + rgba(255,255,255,0.16) border in dark.
  • node.note is the one exception — its warm fill is theme-specific by design. Don't refactor it to a "normal" surface; the warmth is what marks it as commentary.
  • node.star uses the accent (#6b9cf0) in both themes — theme-portable.
  • Sizes are locked. A 40 dia circle in one diagram is a 40 dia circle in every diagram of the set.

02.8Callouts & legend

Two primitives for explanatory chrome — the callout (a warm-tinted note pointing at something) and the legend chip (a colored swatch + label explaining a semantic color used in the diagram).

PrimitiveGeometryUse for
callout.noteSame fill as node.note: warm sable tint (light) / warm near-black (dark), amber stroke in both. 12px rounded.An "aside" anchored to a specific card or arrow. Always paired with a thin line connecting it to its anchor. Used sparingly — 1 per diagram max.

callout + legend · lightwhen explanation belongs in-canvas
Legend is opt-in. Add a legend only when the diagram uses ≥ 4 semantic surface colors that aren't self-evident from icons or labels. A 2-item legend (e.g., "primary" + "secure") is usually noise — drop it and let the icons carry the meaning.
