## What shipped

The final spec of the design-system arc. Two growth surfaces: a 3-screen onboarding carousel for first-time hosted-mode users, and a read-only demo capsule of the partner-vetting run on the auth-free landing page. The onboarding flow covers what dual-research is, how runs work (phase diagram), and how to start exploring (CLI snippet + keyboard shortcuts). The landing page's DemoRunCapsule shows topic, cost, phases, timeline samples, and critique items from a curated JSON fixture, demonstrating the product before sign-in. Completion state persisted to localStorage; reset via `?reset_onboarding=1`.
